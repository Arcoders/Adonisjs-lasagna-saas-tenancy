# Community Code of Conduct

---

## Our Pledge

We, as members, contributors, and leaders of this community, commit to creating an environment where every person feels safe, respected, and valued.

We believe that:

- Every person has inherent dignity and worth
- Our differences in background, identity, and experience are a source of strength, not division
- The quality of our work is inseparable from the quality of our character

We pledge to interact in ways that reflect honesty, humility, and a genuine concern for the well-being of others.

---

## Our Standards

### Behaviors that strengthen our community

- **Truthfulness** – Speak with honesty and clarity. Avoid deception, exaggeration, or hiding important context.
- **Integrity** – Do what you say you will do. Take ownership of your work, your mistakes, and their consequences.
- **Respect** – Treat every person with dignity, regardless of their background, role, or opinion. Listen before responding. Assume good faith.
- **Patience** – Understand that not everyone shares your knowledge, experience, or perspective. Teach with kindness; learn with humility.
- **Gratitude** – Acknowledge the contributions of others. Give credit where it is due. Appreciate the time and effort people offer freely.
- **Justice** – Be fair in your judgments. Do not favor friends or harm rivals. Speak up when you see unfairness.
- **Compassion** – Recognize that everyone faces struggles unseen. Offer help when you can; ask for help when you need it.
- **Excellence** – Strive to do your best, not for recognition, but because good work is its own reward. Let your code, your words, and your actions reflect care and craftsmanship.
- **Humility** – No one knows everything. Be open to being wrong, to learning, and to changing your mind when presented with better evidence.

### Behaviors we do not tolerate

- Dishonesty, misrepresentation, or concealing important facts
- Disrespect, mockery, or belittling others
- Harassment, intimidation, or any form of abuse
- Publishing private information without explicit consent
- Any behavior that damages trust, creates fear, or undermines the safety of others

---

## Our Commitment to Accountability

Community leaders are responsible for upholding these standards. They will:

- Act with fairness and impartiality in all enforcement decisions
- Protect the confidentiality of those who report concerns
- Provide clear reasoning for any moderation action taken

Consequences for violations are proportionate to the impact of the behavior, ranging from a private reminder to a permanent ban, depending on the severity and repetition of the offense.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces—whether online or offline—and also when an individual is officially representing the community in public forums.

## A Final Reflection

A community is not defined by its code alone, but by the character of its people.

In every interaction—whether writing code, reviewing a pull request, or simply replying to a message, we have a choice: to build up or to tear down, to help or to hinder, to act with integrity or to take shortcuts.

We choose to build.

We choose excellence.

We choose kindness.

We choose truth.

This is not just a document. It is a promise we make to each other.
